Scope: Dynamic `/clinic/:slug` specialty detail pages in Operate/Persuade mode.

Audience and job: A patient who has selected a clinical direction needs to understand what the department treats, which services and diagnostics are available, how the visit proceeds, which doctors work there, and how to book.

Direction: A daylight patient pathway that extends the finder-led clinic catalog. Each specialty keeps one accent and one atlas-scale organ image, while open information rails, practical sticky navigation, doctor profiles, and booking actions carry the experience.

Content and constraints: Use existing CMS or localized specialty facts, real doctor profiles, established process steps, and related departments. Do not foreground AI metrics, invent prices or clinical claims, mix student content into the page, or let motion obscure information. Responsive layout, reduced motion, keyboard focus, CMS loading, missing data, and localization are required.
